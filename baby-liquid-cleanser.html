    FAQ

    Questions parents ask.

    How do I clean bottles with it?

    Pour a little onto a washing sponge or bottle brush, clean the bottle, nipple and parts, then rinse under running water for 5–10 seconds.

    Can I wash fruits and vegetables with it?

    Yes. Wash them with the cleanser, then rinse under running water for at least 30 seconds.

    Is it gentle on my hands?

    Yes. It's pH balanced and dermatologically tested for everyday washing.

    Do I still need to sterilise bottles?

    This cleanser is for everyday washing. Follow your paediatrician's advice on sterilising bottles, especially for young babies.
