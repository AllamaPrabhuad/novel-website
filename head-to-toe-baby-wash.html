    FAQ

    Questions parents ask.

    Can I use it on my newborn?

    Yes. It's tear-free, pH 5.5 and soap-free, and made for daily use from the newborn stage.

    Do I need a separate shampoo?

    No. It's a 2-in-1 wash for both hair and body.

    Is it really tear-free?

    The formula is tear-free and gentle on the eyes. Still, keep it away from the eyes where you can, and rinse with clean water if needed.

    How much should I use?

    A small amount is enough. Lather it in your palm, wash gently and rinse well.
