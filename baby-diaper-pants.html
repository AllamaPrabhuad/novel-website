    FAQ

    Questions parents ask.

    Which size should I buy?

    Go by weight: S fits 4–8 kg, M fits 7–12 kg and L fits 9–14 kg. XL and XXL are for bigger babies. If your baby is between sizes, choose the bigger one.

    Are they good for overnight?

    Yes. They absorb for up to 10 hours. Change sooner if the diaper is soiled.

    How do I take off a pant diaper?

    Tear the side seams, slide it off, roll it up and close it with the disposal tape.

    How can I prevent nappy rash?

    Change often, clean gently with a soft wipe, and let the skin air-dry for a minute before the fresh diaper goes on.
