    FAQ

    Questions parents ask.

    How do I use the Tummy Roll-On?

    Roll it gently around your baby's navel and tummy in circular motions.

    From what age can I use it?

    It's made for babies from 0+ months. Do a patch test before the first use.

    Can I use it while my baby sleeps?

    Yes. The roller glides on smoothly, so you can use it without lifting your baby.

    When should I see a doctor?

    If the crying or tummy trouble doesn't settle, or comes with fever, vomiting or anything that worries you, talk to your paediatrician.
