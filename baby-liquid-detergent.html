    FAQ

    Questions parents ask.

    Is it safe for newborn clothes?

    It's made for baby laundry: pH balanced, with an IFRA-certified allergen-free fragrance, and no phosphates, sulphates, parabens, phthalates or dyes.

    How much should I use?

    About one full cap (40 ml) for a 40-litre machine load, or 25 ml in 10 litres of water for hand washing. Use a little more for heavy stains.

    Should I wash baby clothes separately?

    Yes, the pack recommends washing baby clothes separately.

    Is there a refill pack?

    Yes. A refill pack is available on Amazon to top up your bottle.
