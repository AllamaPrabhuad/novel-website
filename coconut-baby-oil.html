    FAQ

    Questions parents ask.

    Can I use it from birth?

    Yes, it's made for babies from 0+ months. Patch-test a little first, and avoid broken or irritated skin.

    What does cold-pressed mean?

    The oil is pressed from fresh coconuts without heat, which helps keep its natural vitamins and nutrients.

    Can I use it on my baby's scalp?

    Yes. It gently conditions the scalp and helps with dryness.

    Why has the oil turned solid?

    That's natural: coconut oil turns solid below about 24°C. Warm the bottle in your hands or in warm water and it melts again.
