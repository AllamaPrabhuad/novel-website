    FAQ

    Questions parents ask.

    Can I use these wipes on my newborn?

    Yes. They're hypoallergenic, pH balanced, alcohol-free and dermatologically tested. For the first weeks or very sensitive skin, many parents prefer our fragrance-free 99% Pure Water wipes.

    Do they have a scent?

    A light aloe vera and chamomile scent. If you'd rather have no scent at all, choose our 99% Pure Water wipes.

    Aloe Vera or Goat Milk: which should I pick?

    Both are gentle everyday wipes. Aloe Vera & Chamomile soothes and hydrates; Goat Milk adds extra nourishment for dry or delicate skin.

    How do I keep the wipes from drying out?

    Press the lid shut after every use and keep the pack away from heat and direct sunlight.

    Can I flush them?

    No. Please throw used wipes in the bin. Flushing wipes can block pipes and drains.
