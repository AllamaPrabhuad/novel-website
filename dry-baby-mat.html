    FAQ

    Questions parents ask.

    Which size do I need?

    Small (70 × 50 cm) suits prams, car seats and travel. Medium (100 × 70 cm) covers a full baby cot. Large (140 × 100 cm) and X-Large (200 × 140 cm) cover more of a 3-foot or 6-foot bed.

    Can I machine wash it?

    Yes. It's machine washable and reusable.

    Is it noisy or plasticky?

    No. It has a soft microfiber top and is noiseless and breathable.

    Can older family members use it too?

    Yes. The larger sizes are handy as bed protection for elders and patients too.
